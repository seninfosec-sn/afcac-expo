About the Expo

About AFCAC

The African Civil Aviation Commission (AFCAC) is the specialized aviation agency of the African Union, established to coordinate, develop, and harmonize civil aviation activities across Africa's 54 member states. Since its inception, AFCAC has championed safe, secure, and sustainable aviation on the continent.

Learn More

AFCAC Expo 2026

The AFCAC Expo 2026 is a premier platform to accelerate sustainable aviation across Africa. Aligned with the Single African Air Transport Market (SAATM) and Agenda 2063, the Expo brings together governments, airlines, airports, technology providers, and international organizations to advance Africa's aviation ecosystem.

Host State – Ethiopia

Ethiopia, home to Addis Ababa — the diplomatic capital of Africa — is the ideal host for this landmark event. With Ethiopian Airlines, one of Africa's fastest-growing carriers, and a booming economy, Ethiopia embodies the spirit of African aviation excellence and ambition.

Getting There

Travel Tips

By Air

Addis Ababa Bole International Airport is one of Africa's busiest hubs, with direct connections to over 100 destinations worldwide via Ethiopian Airlines and multiple international carriers.

Visas

Citizens of most African Union countries enjoy visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to Ethiopia. International delegates should check requirements at their nearest Ethiopian embassy or apply via the e-Visa portal.

Climate

Addis Ababa in November enjoys mild temperatures of 15–25°C with minimal rainfall — perfect conference weather at an altitude of 2,355m. Light layers are recommended for evenings.

Currency

The official currency is the Ethiopian Birr (ETB). USD and EUR are widely accepted at hotels and conference venues. ATMs and currency exchange services are readily available.
